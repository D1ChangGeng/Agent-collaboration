# Review and acceptance decision map

Use a new Review when the candidate or a relevant selected baseline changes.
Inspect the actual candidate through an available Git, native or machine reader,
and report its commit/tree and supporting workspace facts. Verify that recorded
Evidence and receipts describe that candidate and version. Verify reviewer
assignment and independence under the adopted acceptance profile.

An owner WorkItem can start with an omitted or null source_baseline. Use an
eligible intent revision to select, change or clear the work version; retain the
source provenance of Evidence already recorded. Reassess affected Evidence and
Review readiness after a candidate or material work change.

A finding records severity, affected resource, evidence, consequence and
required action. A decision is bounded by its sampling, environment, source
and measured capability. Preserve those bounds when reusing historical
evidence. Query a specific Source archive by its explicit source_id/revision
when that historical material supports the decision.

Acceptance evaluates integrated results, required Reviews and tests,
candidate integrity, protected effects, publication readback and unresolved
items. Record product-owner input separately where the acceptance contract
requires it.

Owner mode keeps these candidate and integrity checks. Natural expiry of a
historical Grant leaves recorded execution and Review facts intact. Explicit
revocation changes eligibility for new actions; shared mode retains current
Grant, Policy, budget and Source readback conditions.

A hash verifies exact bytes. Promote a deployment, native Harness or
cross-machine capability only with evidence from that actual scenario and
configuration.
